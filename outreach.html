What we do on outreach

Good feed only pays when it is used well. Our experts come to your farm to work with you and your workers, so you get better production from the animals you already have.

  • Train workers on silage making
  • Check your feeding formula
  • Show machine use
  • Hay preservation advice
  • Animal health related to feeds
