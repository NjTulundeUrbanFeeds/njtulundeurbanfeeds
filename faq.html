Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for Ugandan farmers

Where are you located?

Our Main Branch is in Tulunde, Kakiri, opposite Petro Oil. Our Mwera Branch is along Hoima Road. We serve farmers nationwide across Uganda.

Do you deliver?

Yes. We deliver in Wakiso and beyond. Delivery cost depends on distance and quantity, so call 0751282930 for a quote.

How do I order?

Open the Products page, add what you need to your cart, enter your name, phone and location, then send the order on WhatsApp. You can also call 0751282930. We confirm stock, price and delivery with you.

Can I order more than one product at once?

Yes. Add as many items as you like to your cart on the Products page and send them together in one WhatsApp order.

Do you sell and hire silage chopping machines?

Both. The 8HP diesel chopping machine is for sale, and you can hire it per day. Ask us about current availability.

What does farm outreach include?

We visit your farm to train workers on silage making, check your feeding formula, show machine use, advise on hay preservation and discuss animal health related to feeds. Transport cost applies.

How long does silage take to be ready?

Properly chopped, packed and sealed silage is usually ready after about three weeks. Our team can guide you through each step.

What is kikanja?

Kikanja is breweries waste used as a low-cost feed for dairy cattle. We sell it dry and fresh.

Are your prices fixed?

The prices on this site are guides and can change. Please call to confirm today's price.
