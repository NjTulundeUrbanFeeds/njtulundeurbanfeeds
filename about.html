About Us

Prepared by Experts

Our Story

NJ Tulunde Urban Feeds started to help Ugandan farmers get more milk and more profit. We are experts in silage making, and we back every sale with practical advice on feeding, machines and storage.

From our branches in Kakiri and Mwera in wakiso on Hoima Road, we supply farmers across Uganda with silage, hay, molasses, livestock microbes, kikanja, animal salts, troughs, milking cans and farm tools. We also hire out silage chopping machines and visit farms to train workers.

Our goal is simple: when your animals eat better, your farm earns more.

Vision

To become a trusted and leading provider of quality animal feeds and farm solutions that improve livestock farming and farmers' income.

Mission

To produce and supply affordable, high-quality animal feeds and farm products that promote healthy animals, better production and customer satisfaction.

Our Objectives

  • Provide quality feeds and farm products to farmers.
  • Improve livestock productivity through better nutrition.
  • Build strong and lasting relationships with customers.
  • Expand the business and reach more farmers in different areas.
  • Create employment opportunities and support community development.
  • Maintain honesty, quality and professionalism in all services.

Core Values

Quality. We provide products that meet high standards and satisfy farmers' needs.

Integrity. We operate with honesty, transparency and fairness.

Customer care. We value our customers and serve them with respect and commitment.

Innovation. We continuously improve our products and farming solutions.

Teamwork. We work together to achieve success and growth.

Reliability. We deliver products and services that farmers can trust.

Sustainability. We support farming practices that protect the environment and future generations.
